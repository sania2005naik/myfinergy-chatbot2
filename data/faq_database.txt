Q: What is myFinergy?
A: myFinergy is a sales-technology platform designed for financial advisors to help them move from product-based selling to goal-based financial planning.

Q: Who can use myFinergy?
A: Insurance advisors, mutual fund distributors, financial advisors, wealth advisors and other professionals who help clients plan their financial goals can use myFinergy.

Q: What problem does myFinergy solve?
A: It helps advisors identify qualified prospects, start meaningful financial conversations, conduct financial diagnostics, identify financial gaps, present goals visually, generate financial opportunities, follow up systematically, and improve client engagement and conversion.

Q: Is myFinergy an insurance-selling app?
A: No. myFinergy is designed around financial goals and client needs, rather than starting with a particular financial product.

Q: Why should I use a goal-based approach?
A: Clients generally buy more confidently when they understand what financial problem they are solving rather than simply being presented with a product.

Q: Do I need to be a financial planning expert to use myFinergy?
A: No. myFinergy simplifies much of the financial assessment and presentation process so advisors can conduct structured conversations.

Q: How do I log in?
A: Use your registered mobile number/credentials and follow the login process provided by myFinergy.

Q: What should I do if I forget my password?
A: Use the password recovery/reset option available on the login screen.

Q: What should I do if I don't receive the OTP?
A: Check your mobile network and verify that the registered mobile number is correct. If the OTP still doesn't arrive, contact support.

Q: Can I use myFinergy for multiple clients?
A: Yes. The CRM is designed to manage multiple prospects and clients.

Q: Where do I find my prospects?
A: Go to CRM → Leads.

Q: How do I add a new prospect?
A: Add the prospect through the CRM/client onboarding process and enter the available basic information.

Q: What information should I collect initially?
A: Start with basic information such as Name, Mobile number, Age, Occupation, Income/salary, Family information, Existing financial commitments, and Financial goals.

Q: Do I need complete client information before starting?
A: Not necessarily. You can begin with basic information and progressively complete the client's profile.

Q: Why is monthly income important?
A: Income helps establish the client's financial capacity and provides context for goals, savings, protection and investment requirements.

Q: What is the best way to introduce myFinergy to a prospect?
A: Don't begin by saying, "I want to sell you insurance/investments." Instead, position the conversation around: "Let's check your financial fitness and see whether your current financial plan is strong enough to achieve your important goals."

Q: What if the prospect says, "I already have an advisor"?
A: Use myFinergy as a second-opinion financial fitness assessment, rather than attacking the existing advisor.

Q: What if the prospect says, "I don't need financial planning"?
A: Explain that the purpose is to check whether their existing financial arrangements are aligned with their future goals.

Q: What if the prospect says, "I don't have time"?
A: Offer a short initial diagnostic conversation and explain that the platform is designed to simplify the process.

Q: What if the prospect says, "Send me the details on WhatsApp"?
A: Use the appropriate myFinergy presentation/report/link to continue the conversation digitally.

Q: What is a financial diagnostic?
A: It is an assessment designed to identify how financially prepared a client is for their important future goals and financial risks.

Q: What is FinFit?
A: FinFit represents the client's financial fitness/readiness based on the information and goals captured in myFinergy.

Q: What is the Finergy Score?
A: The Finergy Score is a simplified indicator that helps communicate the client's financial readiness/strength.

Q: Why should I show a score to the client?
A: A score makes a complex financial situation easier to understand and gives the advisor a natural starting point for discussion.

Q: What should I do if the client's score is low?
A: Don't immediately sell a product. First identify why the score is low, which financial gap is responsible, which goal is affected, and what action can improve the situation.

Q: What is the FinFit presentation?
A: It is a client-facing financial fitness presentation that helps the advisor explain the client's financial position and goals visually.

Q: How do I generate the FinFit presentation?
A: The typical workflow is: CRM → Leads → View Details → Edit/update information → Goals → Add Goals → FinFit Presentation → Share/Send PPT.

Q: Why should I share the FinFit presentation before selling?
A: Because the presentation creates financial awareness and problem recognition before the advisor recommends solutions.

Q: Why are goals important in myFinergy?
A: Goals transform an abstract financial conversation into a specific financial requirement.

Q: What types of goals can I add?
A: Examples include Children's education, Children's marriage, Home purchase, Vehicle purchase, Retirement, Wealth creation, Emergency fund, Travel, Business, and other major financial aspirations.

Q: What is "Treatment" in myFinergy?
A: Treatment should be viewed as the recommended action plan for addressing an identified financial gap.

Q: What is the difference between Diagnosis and Treatment?
A: Diagnosis answers "What is the financial problem?" while Treatment answers "What should the client do about it?"

Q: Should treatment always mean selling a product?
A: No. Treatment should first address the client's financial need. A financial product may be one component of the solution.

Q: How do I identify an opportunity?
A: Look for gaps created by Income risk, Protection requirements, Health-cost exposure, Retirement requirements, Education goals, Existing liabilities, Investment requirements, or Goal funding shortages.

Q: What is the Revenue section?
A: The Revenue section helps the advisor understand potential business opportunities emerging from identified client needs.

Q: How do I avoid appearing sales-focused?
A: Lead with: Goal → Gap → Impact → Recommendation → Action, rather than Product → Features → Premium → Closing.

Q: What should I say when starting the FinFit conversation?
A: A simple opening: "Before we discuss any financial product, let's first understand whether your current financial plan is strong enough to achieve the goals you've set for yourself."

Q: What should I ask after showing the score?
A: Ask: "Were you expecting this score?" This creates a natural conversation.

Q: What if the client becomes worried after seeing a low score?
A: Reassure them: "The purpose of this assessment isn't to worry you. It is to identify the areas we can improve before they become bigger problems."

Q: What should I do after sharing the FinFit report?
A: Follow the CRM's recommended next action and continue the conversation based on the client's response.

Q: What should I send as a follow-up?
A: Examples include Financial awareness content, Wisdom shots, Short educational videos/reels, Client testimonials, Community invitations, Financial insights, and Relevant diagnostic information.

Q: Can I share myFinergy content through WhatsApp?
A: Yes, wherever WhatsApp sharing is enabled.

Q2: What is the Financial Test?
A: The test helps collect information required to understand the client's financial position and readiness.

Q: What is the recommended myFinergy workflow?
A: Lead → Connect → Educate → Test → Diagnose → Present → Identify Gap → Treat → Follow-up → Track.

Q: What does T1 mean?
A: T1 – Teach: Educate the prospect and create financial awareness.

Q: What does T2 mean?
A: T2 – Test: Conduct the financial assessment.

Q: What does T3 mean?
A: T3 – Treat: Identify the gap and recommend appropriate action.

Q: What does T4 mean?
A: T4 – Track: Continue monitoring, follow-up and client engagement.

Q: How can myFinergy save an advisor's time?
A: It can reduce manual calculations, simplify presentation creation and provide structured workflows.

Q: Does myFinergy guarantee sales?
A: No. It is a sales-enablement and financial-planning technology platform. Results depend on advisor execution, client quality, communication and follow-up.

Q: How can myFinergy help me become a premium advisor?
A: By changing the advisor's positioning from "I sell financial products" to "I help clients identify and solve financial gaps."

Q: What is the biggest mistake advisors make?
A: Starting with the product instead of the client's financial problem.

Q: The app isn't loading. What should I do?

Q: A button isn't working. What should I do?

: My client isn't receiving the WhatsApp message. What should I check?
